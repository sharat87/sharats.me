---
title: Human gates
---

Once, we had,

> This webpage only works with Internet Explorer.

Then came:

> This webpage only works with Google Chrome.

Now it is

> This webpage only works with humans.

When set on a task, poor LLMs are having to resort to delegating parts of the job to humans. Because some webpages have so much bot protection, that they can't do it themselves (not without burning another mountain of tokens that is).

Where I used to set an agent on a long running, or overnight task, my worry used to be that the LLM would get stuck midway, or veer offcourse. Now, I'm almost certain it'll hit something that's _human gated_.

I then hate all that. Why can't my agent be let in? It's acting under my instruction... or so I believe.

And then I realize my own blog is behing Cloudflare.

Oh well. Back to being the shepherd of agents. I thought by end of 2026, I won't have to babysit agents as much, but doesn't look like it's happening.
